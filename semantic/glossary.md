# Glossary

## Net settled amount

Sum of current completion, line, and reversal amounts for fleet card purchases. Authorization holds are excluded after a completion supersedes them.

## Transaction

One fleet card event: auth, line, completion, or reversal. The curated key is the source system plus the source event id.

## Benefits claim

An HSA, FSA, or COBRA claim. Member identifiers and claim categories are health-context fields and are not returned on a person.

## Purchase group

Auth, extra lines, completion, and reversal that commit together for one purchase id.
